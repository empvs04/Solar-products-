Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about product enquiries, bulk purchasing, and distributorship.

You can submit your enquiry using the contact form on this page by selecting the relevant product category, or call our customer support lines directly at 90531 10707 and 81990 36060. Our team will provide detailed product specifications, availability, and pricing.
For bulk commercial, industrial, or project orders, select "Bulk Order" in the enquiry form above and mention your required quantity and location in the message. You may also contact our team directly at info@vidhyutsaathi.com or call our official phone lines.
We actively welcome channel partners, regional distributors, and dealers across India. Select "Distributorship Enquiry" in the contact form, provide your company details and operational region, or click "Buy Distributorship" to review our partner program.
To help us assist you quickly, please include your name, contact phone number, location or city, the specific product category or model you are interested in, and an estimated quantity if you are enquiring about business or bulk procurement.
