Return, Refund & Replacement Policy

Clear policies and transparent guidance on returns, replacements, and refunds.

Website: https://vidhyutsaathi.in/

Brand: Vidhyut Saathi

Effective Date: 10 November 2026

1. Introduction

At Vidhyut Saathi, customer satisfaction is important to us. We aim to supply quality solar lighting, LED products, electrical testing instruments, energy saving products and other electrical solutions.

This policy explains the conditions under which customers may request a return, replacement or refund for products purchased through our website.

2. Return Eligibility

Customers may request a return or replacement in the following situations:

  1. The product received is damaged during transportation.
  2. The product received is defective or non-functional.
  3. The wrong product, model or variant has been delivered.
  4. The product is materially different from its description on our website.
  5. The product is incomplete or missing essential accessories that were included in the order.
  6. Any other circumstance in which a return, replacement or refund is required under applicable consumer protection laws.
Return Request Period

Customers should raise a return or replacement request within 7 days of delivery.

For visible transit damage, customers are encouraged to notify us within 48 hours of delivery and provide photographs or an unboxing video, if available.

Failure to provide an unboxing video will not automatically invalidate an otherwise legitimate complaint.

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights available under applicable law.

3. Conditions for Returns

Where a return is requested, the following conditions generally apply:

  • The product should be returned with its original packaging, wherever reasonably possible.
  • All accessories, manuals and components supplied with the product should be included.
  • The product should not have been intentionally damaged, modified or misused.
  • The product should be accompanied by the order number or proof of purchase.
  • Products showing signs of installation, use or handling will be assessed based on the nature of the complaint.
  • A product will not be rejected merely because its original packaging has been opened when inspection or reasonable examination was necessary to identify a defect.

4. Non-Returnable Situations

Subject to applicable consumer rights, the following situations may not qualify for a change-of-mind return:

  • Products damaged due to improper installation or handling by the customer.
  • Products damaged due to unauthorised modification or repair.
  • Products damaged due to misuse, negligence or accidental damage after delivery.
  • Products returned without essential components or accessories due to customer loss.
  • Products with normal wear and tear after use.
  • Products returned solely because the customer changed their mind, where no return right is provided by the applicable product-specific return terms.

These exclusions do not apply to products that are defective, damaged on arrival, incorrectly supplied, materially misdescribed or otherwise eligible for a remedy under applicable law.

5. How to Request a Return or Replacement

To request a return, replacement or refund:

Step 1: Contact our customer support team.
Email: business.vidhyutsaathi@gmail.com

Step 2: Provide the following information:

  • Customer name.
  • Order number.
  • Product name.
  • Date of delivery.
  • Reason for the return or replacement.
  • Relevant photographs or videos, if available.

Step 3: Our team will review the request and provide return instructions.

Step 4: If approved, the product may be collected through our logistics partner or the customer may be instructed to return it through an agreed shipping method.

Please do not send products to our office without first receiving return instructions.

6. Damaged or Defective Products

If a product arrives damaged, defective or non-functional, please contact us as soon as possible.

Our team may request photographs, videos or reasonable technical information to assess the complaint.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, we may provide:

  • A replacement product.
  • Repair or service support.
  • A refund.
  • Another legally appropriate remedy agreed with the customer.

7. Refund Policy

Refunds may be issued in the following circumstances:

  • A return is approved.
  • A replacement cannot reasonably be provided.
  • An order is cancelled before dispatch and the payment has already been received.
  • An order cannot be fulfilled by us.
  • A refund is otherwise required under applicable law.
Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved:

  • Prepaid orders: Refunds will generally be processed to the original payment method.
  • UPI and online payments: Refunds will be initiated through the relevant payment provider.
  • Cash on Delivery orders: Customers may be asked to provide verified bank account or UPI details for the refund.

Refunds will generally be initiated within 7–10 business days after approval and completion of any required return verification.

Actual crediting time may depend on the payment provider or bank.

Any longer processing period required by applicable law will not be relied upon to delay a legally due refund.

8. Replacement Policy

Where a replacement is approved:

  • Replacement will be subject to product availability.
  • We will aim to dispatch the replacement within a reasonable period after approval.
  • If the same product is unavailable, we will discuss an appropriate alternative or refund with the customer.

9. Cancellation Policy

Customers may request cancellation before an order is dispatched.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible through the normal cancellation process. The customer may instead request a return where eligible.

We will not impose cancellation charges contrary to applicable law.

If we cancel an order due to stock unavailability, pricing errors or operational reasons, any amount received for that order will be refunded.

10. Return Shipping Charges

Where the return or replacement is due to:

  • A defective product.
  • Transit damage.
  • Incorrect product delivery.
  • An error attributable to Vidhyut Saathi.

Reasonable return shipping costs will be borne by Vidhyut Saathi.

For other eligible returns, return shipping arrangements and costs will be communicated to the customer before the return is initiated.

11. Warranty

Certain products may carry a manufacturer or seller warranty.

Warranty duration, coverage and exclusions will be determined by the applicable product listing, warranty card or accompanying documentation.

Customers should retain their invoice and warranty documentation for future claims.

Warranty coverage does not replace statutory consumer rights.

12. Important Information

  • Please check your delivery address and contact information before placing an order.
  • Product specifications and availability may vary by model or variant.
  • Refund approval will be based on the circumstances and applicable policy.
  • No provision in this policy is intended to waive or restrict mandatory consumer rights.
