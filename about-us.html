About Vidhyut Saathi

At Vidhyut Saathi, we believe that smarter energy choices create a brighter and more sustainable future. We are committed to making energy-efficient electrical and solar products accessible to both individual customers and businesses, helping them discover practical solutions for their everyday energy needs.

From homes looking for efficient lighting to businesses seeking dependable products for commercial use, retail, and bulk purchasing, Vidhyut Saathi aims to serve a diverse range of customers with solutions designed for modern energy requirements.

Our product portfolio includes solar lights, LED lights, LED bulbs, clamp meters, solar chargers, smart power strips, and a growing range of energy-saving and electrical products.
