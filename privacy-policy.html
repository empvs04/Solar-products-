Privacy Policy

Transparent data handling and privacy protection for visitors, customers, and partners.

Website: https://vidhyutsaathi.in/

Brand: Vidhyut Saathi

Effective Date: 10 November 2026

Last Updated: 10 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vidhyut Saathi ("we", "our", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of visitors, customers, dealers, distributors and business partners who use our website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and protect your personal information when you visit our website, purchase products, submit enquiries or communicate with us.

By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information
  • Full name.
  • Mobile number.
  • Email address.
  • Billing address.
  • Shipping and delivery address.
  • City, state and PIN code.
B. Order and Transaction Information
  • Products purchased.
  • Order details and order history.
  • Payment status and transaction references.
  • Cash on Delivery information.
  • Refund and replacement details.

We do not intend to store complete debit card, credit card, UPI PIN or banking passwords on our servers. Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers.

C. Business Enquiry Information

If you apply for a dealership, distributorship or channel partnership, we may collect:

  • Business or company name.
  • Business address.
  • Contact person's name.
  • Mobile number and email address.
  • Business experience and relevant commercial information.
D. Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain information may automatically be collected, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Operating system.
  • Website usage information.
  • Pages visited and time spent on the website.
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  1. Processing and fulfilling customer orders.
  2. Arranging product shipping and delivery.
  3. Sending order confirmations and updates.
  4. Providing customer support.
  5. Processing refunds, replacements and returns.
  6. Responding to dealership and distributorship enquiries.
  7. Improving our products, services and website.
  8. Preventing fraud and unauthorised transactions.
  9. Maintaining business, tax and accounting records.
  10. Sending promotional communications where permitted and, where required, with your consent.
  11. Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

However, we may share relevant information with trusted service providers when necessary, including:

  • Courier and logistics companies for product delivery.
  • Payment gateways and financial service providers for payment processing.
  • Website hosting and technology providers.
  • Customer support and communication service providers.
  • Accounting, auditing and professional advisers.
  • Government authorities, courts or law enforcement agencies when legally required.

Such sharing will be limited to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember shopping cart items.
  • Improve website functionality.
  • Understand website traffic.
  • Improve user experience.
  • Support website security.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Certain website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

Where legally required, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, misuse or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or internet storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • Processing orders and providing services.
  • Maintaining financial and tax records.
  • Handling customer complaints and warranty claims.
  • Preventing fraud.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws and their effective dates, you may have rights to:

  • Request information about personal data processed by us.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a grievance regarding the processing of your personal information.
  • Nominate another person to exercise applicable rights in circumstances permitted by law.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, payment platforms or external services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies before providing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for use by individuals who are legally capable of entering into transactions.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. Where parental or guardian consent is legally required, appropriate safeguards will be followed.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our business practices, technology or applicable laws.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
